## 3. What we can make

### Brand and campaigns
Give the business a look people can recognise. We create visual identities and campaign ideas, then take them into the places people meet you: packaging, launch materials, social posts and outdoor advertising. Your team gets a system it can keep using.

### Websites
Help people understand what you do and see why it matters. We shape the story, design the pages and create the product visuals. Development can be included, with the platform, content and handover agreed together, so your team knows how to run the site.

### Digital products
Make the important things feel straightforward. We design flows, interfaces, prototypes and design systems with your product and engineering teams. Bring us a new product, a feature that needs work or a workflow that needs untangling.

## 4. How we get there

Start with a focused sprint, bring us a full project, or keep us close for ongoing work. We'll work out the right shape together.

### Find the real brief
We get close to the business, its audience and what needs to change. Then we agree the scope, fee, schedule and review points. A sprint can explore one direction or prototype first; full production is a separate scope.

### Give you something to react to
We develop the concepts, layouts or prototypes your project calls for. You see the work and the reasoning, discuss it with the people making it, and help shape the direction at the agreed reviews.

### Finish it properly
Once the direction is right, we work through the details and hand over the finished work, files and guidance. If we're staying on, we plan the next priorities and the capacity to deliver them.

## 6. A few things worth asking

### What should we budget?
Tell us what you have in mind and share a range if you have one. We'll recommend a scope and fee, with clear deliverables, reviews and payment milestones. You'll know the commitment before work begins.

### Can you work to our deadline?
Share it at the start. We'll tell you what's realistic and propose a schedule around the work, content and approvals, including time for production and handover.

### Do you work with agencies?
Yes. Bring us into a client project as a named partner or under your agency's brand. We'll agree responsibilities, client contact and how the collaboration is presented before starting.

### Can you work on just one part?
Absolutely. An identity, a website, a product feature or a particular design question. We'll work out how our part fits with your team, including the handoff to your developers or other collaborators.
